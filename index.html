Who I am

        

          

            
I'm an IT Technician at GZ Industries South Africa, where I secure and monitor infrastructure for a 200+ user manufacturing environment: Fortinet firewalls, Wazuh SIEM, Microsoft 365, Azure and Intune.

            
I started as an intern in February 2025 and was appointed IT Technician in February 2026. Along the way I built a PowerShell network scanning tool for asset discovery. That work gave me a defender's view of real networks: their attack surface, their weak spots, and how things actually fail.

            
Now I'm moving into ethical hacking and offensive security. I'm building those skills through the Cisco Ethical Hacker course, TryHackMe, Kali Linux and Python/PowerShell scripting.

            
"To defend a network well, you have to understand how it gets attacked."
